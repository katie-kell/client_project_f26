Campus Partner Resources

Find What You Need

You do not have to figure everything out on your own. U-M has resources that can help you plan your project, work with your team, communicate with partners, and handle challenges along the way.

Start with what you need help with and find a resource that can point you in the right direction.

I Need Help Planning My Project

If your team is still figuring out the scope, timeline, or responsibilities, start here.

  • Project Scoping Handout & Terms - Figure out what your project should include and what might be outside of your scope.
  • Project Management Worksheet - Organize your project goals, team roles, and timeline.
  • Project Manager Guide - Get help managing responsibilities and keeping your project moving.

I Need Help Working With a Community

Working with a community or organization means thinking beyond what your team wants to accomplish. These resources can help you think about your role and the impact of your work.

  • Social Identity Snapshot - Think about how your own identity and experiences might affect the way you approach the project.
  • "Check Yourself" Community Engagement Checklist - Check your assumptions before working with a community or partner.
  • IAP2 Spectrum of Public Participation - Learn about different levels of community involvement and decision-making.

I Need Help Leading My Team

Leading a project does not mean doing everything yourself. These resources can help you understand your responsibilities and keep your team organized.

  • Student Organization Officer Guide - Learn more about the expectations and responsibilities of student leaders.
  • Project Manager Guide - Get guidance for managing your team and project.
  • Project Manager Commitment Form - Understand what you are agreeing to when taking on a project leadership role.

I Need More Support

Sometimes the resource you need is another person. If your team is stuck, confused about expectations, or dealing with something you are not sure how to handle, reach out instead of waiting until it becomes a bigger problem.

  • UMSI Engaged Learning - Learn more about engaged learning programs and opportunities at UMSI.
  • Engaged Learning Office - Learn more about ELO and the support they provide.
  • Contact the Engaged Learning Office - Reach out directly if you have a question or need help.

Not Sure Where to Start?

Go back to the stage of your experience you are currently in. If your project has not started yet, begin with Preparing. If you are starting to work with your team and partner, head to Starting Your Experience.
